Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Live Photo Collage, available at photowall.princejose.dev ("the app"), lets you sign in with Google to get your own private photo board for an event, and share a QR code so guests can upload photos without an account of their own. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What the app collects

  • Google account info — when you sign in, the app reads your Google profile (name, email, profile picture) to create or look up your board.
  • Phone number (optional) — you may add a phone number to your own profile; it's stored alongside your account and is never required.
  • Uploaded photos — stored on the app's server, with ownership of each photo tracked so it only ever belongs to the board it was uploaded to. Guests upload without signing in, and no personal data is collected from them beyond the photo file itself.

Who can see your board

Only you and any admin of the app can view, download, or clear your board's photos — no other user of the app can see it. Guests who scan your QR code can upload to your board but cannot view it or any other board.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not include any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or third-party tracker.
  • It does not share photos, profile data, or phone numbers between boards.
  • It does not sell or share your data with third parties, other than Google for sign-in.

Your controls

You can download every photo on your board as a zip at any time, and clear your board's photos whenever you want to start fresh for the next event.

Google Sign-In

Sign-in uses Google's own Sign-In flow. Google's handling of the credentials involved is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the issue tracker.
